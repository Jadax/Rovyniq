# Rovyniq product direction

## Positioning

Rovyniq earns trust through an original, calm and evidence-led experience—not competitor copying, lock-in, hidden defaults or unsupported automation. It helps people understand their ITR12 before they file.

## Product promise

- Every estimate is labelled as an estimate.
- Every material figure can show its source, calculation and review state.
- Sensitive actions are deliberate and reversible where possible.
- Complex situations surface practitioner review instead of false certainty.

## Experience pillars

1. **Calm:** one task at a time, gentle progress, short plain-language explanations and no alarmist urgency.
2. **Clarity:** a single tax breakdown shows income, deductions, taxable income, normal tax, credits, PAYE and estimated balance; each line has evidence/provenance.
3. **Confidence:** evidence links, clear review status, immutable taxpayer approval, strong security and visible limitations.
4. **Momentum:** mobile-first capture and questions; document upload only after secure ingestion controls are operational.

## Platform sequence

| Stage | Delivery |
|---|---|
| Website preview | Public explanation and private-preview registration; no tax data |
| Private web beta | Authenticated workspace, secure document ingestion, adaptive questions, tax breakdown, manual SARS handoff |
| Mobile app | Reuses the same API/domain contracts; camera capture, document upload and question flow—not a separate tax engine |
| Provider integrations | Only official, consent-based and contract-tested providers |

## Non-negotiable reliability controls

Availability/error budgets, encrypted backups with restore drills, idempotent jobs, queues/outbox, audit trails, tenant-isolation tests, rate limits, WAF/DDoS protection, incident runbook, vulnerability management, independent penetration testing and practitioner review before public filing.
