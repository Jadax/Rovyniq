Good questions

No clever fine print.

Does Rovyniq submit directly to SARS?

Not currently. Rovyniq prepares a review package and controlled manual eFiling handoff. It will never claim automated SARS submission without an authorised, documented SARS integration.

Will it replace a tax practitioner?

Complex circumstances may need a registered tax practitioner. Rovyniq highlights situations that deserve professional review rather than presenting uncertainty as advice.

Can I use it now?

Rovyniq is currently a private pilot. You can create an account, and access is confirmed before any taxpayer documents are accepted.
