Guided preparation

What we will ask you

Start with the sections that apply to you. Rovyniq will turn these into saved, reviewable questions after document extraction is connected. This guide is not a substitute for the official SARS return or professional advice.

  1. 01

    About you and your household

    Confirm your tax residency, contact details, marital status, dependants and any medical-aid members.

    • Did your personal or family circumstances change during the year?
    • Were you a South African tax resident for the full year?
    • Do you need to include medical-aid dependants?
  2. 02

    Employment and benefits

    Add every IRP5 or IT3(a), including certificates from jobs you changed or left.

    • Did you earn salary, a bonus, commission, allowance or fringe benefit?
    • Did you receive a travel, subsistence or home-office allowance?
    • Did you have more than one employer?
  3. 03

    Investments and other income

    Add certificates and statements for interest, dividends, capital gains, rental income, foreign income and business activity.

    • Did you receive interest, dividends or investment distributions?
    • Did you sell investments, property or other capital assets?
    • Did you earn rental, freelance, business or foreign income?
  4. 04

    Contributions, claims and deductions

    Collect retirement, medical, donation, travel and other supporting records before making any claim.

    • Did you make retirement-annuity contributions outside payroll?
    • Do you have medical expenses or a medical tax certificate?
    • Do you have donation, travel-logbook or work-expense evidence that may be relevant?
  5. 05

    Review before submission

    Compare every material amount to its source, resolve missing records and approve the final return yourself.

    • Does each important amount have a document or clear explanation?
    • Have you checked prior-year information and any provisional-tax obligations?
    • Are you ready to review the final calculation before manual SARS eFiling?
